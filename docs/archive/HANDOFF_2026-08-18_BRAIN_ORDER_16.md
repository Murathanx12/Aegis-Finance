## 2. Adopted with amendments

- **Item 3, AEGIS-NET-1 — the barrier head is a PATH statistic.** §65: a
  forward-power projection is only valid for a statistic that does not depend on
  window length, and `P(+20% before −10%)` is a path extremum twice over. So:
  the barrier head trains and screens like every other head, but its evaluation
  is measured ON the forward horizon (never scaled from history), its labels are
  checked against §15 (the barrier pair IS a trailing-stop structure — the label
  must not encode the trap), and no forward-power extrapolation from historical
  MDE is admissible for it. The tournament (5 archs × heads) is one
  pre-registered screen batch: BH-FDR with m = every cell run, **shared
  resamples across cells** (unshared draws measured the RNG once already),
  k_eff printed. §37 applies to the first architecture that "wins": check the
  pass as hard as a kill, and reproducibility is a property of the PREDICTIONS
  (the LightGBM seed lesson — declare the tolerance; NN training is less
  deterministic than the histogram summation that already burned us).
- **Item 7, EXTREME-MOVE-LIBRARY-1 — adopted, with §62 load-bearing.** The
  Boudoukh continuation/reversal split is the right first historical test and is
  tagged `hypothesis_source` (caps at `ADAPTIVE_HISTORICAL_VALIDATION`). Every
  cell reports its tradable fraction and arrival axis from row one — a
  continuation effect that lives in the overnight gap is verdict-family three
  (real, observable, UNREACHABLE), and we already found 87–95% of the earnings
  effect there. No ratio reported when its denominator is below its own MDE.
- **Item 8, EXPECTATION-DISLOCATION-1 — same §62 condition**, plus: the

  the lower turnover, not that the cost line shrank. Target-LEVEL signals stay
  dead; nothing here reopens them.
- **Item 9, the relationship graph — the gate is DATA, not architecture.**
  Second-order lag is already on Brain's own list and MARKET-GRAPH-1 H1 is our

  edge data source and its PIT audit FIRST; no graph model is built until the
  edges pass the same provenance bar as prices. Capponi et al. is
  `hypothesis_source`.
- **Item 10, Track E — adopted as amended by GPT itself** (conditional

  denominators), not a new metric.

## 3. The collisions the plan could not have known about

**(a) Item 4, the conditional router, walks into G5.** G5 has **three receipts,
all negative: a learned conditional SHAPE adds nothing even given an oracle
scale**, and the roadmap's own conclusion is that headroom is in scale, not
shape. A mixture-of-experts that learns "when each signal matters" is precisely
a learned conditional shape. This does not forbid the router — scope-aware
verdicts cut both ways — but its registration must (i) cite the three G5
receipts as its corpse-control, (ii) state exactly which conditional claim is
distinct from what G5 tested, and (iii) pre-declare the contrast against the
unconditional mixture (which GPT itself requires for strategies — apply it to
the router's own existence). **If the router cannot state what G5 did not test,
it is not built.** The plan's sentence "the router tests the conditional claim
against the unconditional corpse" is the right rule; G5 is the corpse it must
be tested against, by name.

**(b) One `decide()` entry point (§63).** The factory's
REJECTED/SHELF/PROMISING states are assigned by the existing Gym verdict
machinery — screen BH-FDR at m = tests run, export Holm at the declared budget —
never by a second criterion picker grown inside the factory. The factory is a
QUEUE around `decide()`, not a rival to it.

**(c) The Learning Brain trains on our data ⇒ direction checks, never alpha
claims.** Nothing AEGIS-NET produces touches capital except through the
registry's support ladder and forward lanes. "If LightGBM wins, LightGBM gets
the capital" — amended: *whichever wins gets the LANE, and the lane gets the
24 months.*

**(d) Terminal wealth is the OBJECTIVE, not the OUTCOME METRIC.** §59: a
terminal-return claim costs ~95 years to confirm. The portfolio layer optimizes
declared-utility terminal wealth (that is what an objective is for); the
*evidence* for whether it works is stated on risk outcomes and relative claims,
where the same data resolves ~30× faster. The plan's own sentence — "subject to
an explicit probability of unacceptable loss" — is the declared-λ machinery,
and λ is DECLARED, never fitted (§ the bounded-sacrifice rule; and the
personalities remain declared preferences, Order 14 §5).

**(e) The factory's LLM step is bounded by A11.** "Ask the LLM for economic
mechanisms" is `hypothesis_source` work — measured role
`PRESENTATION_AND_RESEARCH_ASSISTANCE`, and every mechanism it proposes gets
the same `distinct_from`/corpse screen as a human idea. The LLM narrates and
proposes; the engine computes and decides.
