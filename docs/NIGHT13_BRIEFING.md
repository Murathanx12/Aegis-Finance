## 1. TRANSACTION-ENSEMBLE — the honest substitute for his records

Generate N transaction histories **consistent with every known anchor**: the
two PIT sheets (prices at 2025-11-07 and 2026-01-13, sold-at annotations),
the conviction decision log, book_lanes, the +73.7%/+$15,165 brokerage
figure, the "115%" header, current holdings incl. QUBT 300. Run
CONVICTION-REPLAY's decomposition over the whole ensemble.

- A conclusion that holds across the ensemble is ADOPTED (labelled
  `ensemble_robust`).
- A conclusion that flips within the ensemble is labelled `DATA_NEEDED`
  with the **minimal exact ask** attached (e.g. "broker CSV export,
  ~2 minutes, resolves items 3 and 5").
- Never collapse the ensemble to a preferred member. The range IS the
  result. This replaces "choose the best outcome and validate it".

## 3. BOOK-KEYED EXPOSURE CONTROL v1 — the answer to "sell before the war"

NIGHT-12 proved the market-keyed controller correctly never fired (SPY only
fell 8.9%) while Murat's book fell 22.9% at beta 2.15 — **a sizing problem**.
The instrument that gets "somewhere close to the line" without forecasting
wars: **volatility targeting + a beta budget + a drawdown-responsive ladder
with pre-specified re-entry**, keyed to the BOOK, not the index. It trims
mechanically as book vol/beta expands and re-adds as it contracts — exit and
re-entry are one rule, so the buy-the-dip half is built in, imperfectly but
automatically.

Discipline: the Iran-war dip is ONE episode — a rule built to fix it must
not be tuned on it. Calibrate on the long panel's drawdown episodes
(1962→: '74, '87, '00–'02, '08, '20, '22) and hold the war episode OUT as
the test case. Report, per episode: drawdown avoided, bull participation
kept (both captures), turnover cost, and terminal wealth vs unmanaged.
Pre-register; power-check; §19 on every number.

## 4. PICKS × MANAGEMENT factorial — his claim, tested as a matrix
