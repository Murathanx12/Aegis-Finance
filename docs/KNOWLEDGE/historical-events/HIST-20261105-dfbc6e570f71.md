# Asia financial pressure and resilience coexist

**Summary.** The BOJ's Oct 23 financial-system report names rate, valuation and foreign non-bank financing vulnerabilities while reporting functioning intermediation and adequate bank buffers in its scenarios. A September BOK stability briefing reports higher yields/spreads and weaker non-bank borrowers alongside broadly sound banks and external funding. A Nov 3 KSSK/BI release reports tighter liquidity and outflows alongside Q3 financial-system stability. This Nov 5 synthesis is a retrospective lesson: pressure is observed, but these sources do not establish a regional banking crisis or direct U.S. issuer loss.
