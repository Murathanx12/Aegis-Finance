- Bigdata.com MCP returned `outOfCredits` on every call this session (find_securities, events_calendar) — **zero Bigdata.com data in this report**; all catalyst dates and analyst actions below come from Exa web search of company IR calendars, exchange filings (SEC/HKEX/TSE/DART), and sell-side/news aggregators (MarketBeat, MarketScreener, TipRanks, FXEmpire, Investing.com), captured 2026-09-25.
- Dates marked **[CONFIRMED]** come from a company IR press release/financial calendar. Dates marked **[EST.]** are extrapolated from the prior year's/quarter's reporting cadence (typical for names that "do not pre-announce," esp. Korea/Taiwan) — treat as a 3–5 trading-day window, not a fixed date, until confirmed closer to the event.
- "WLS?" column is a judgment call (large/mid/small cap of a developed or EM listing = likely yes; A-share/STAR Market or thin free-float = flagged for verification), not a lookup against the actual WLS constituent file — **verify every name against the live Bloomberg WLS list before sizing a book**, since this determines contest eligibility, not just liquidity.
- Market caps are rounded, order-of-magnitude, as of ~Sept 2026 (not point-sourced per name); ADV notes are qualitative (mega/large/liquid vs. thinner).
