# AEGIS Strategic Invariants

Read this before changing research priorities. This is intentionally short and stable; detailed experiments live in roadmap/finding docs.

1. **Objective:** maximize expected compound return / P&L subject to explicit survival constraints. Risk is a budget for measured asymmetric opportunity, not a goal to minimize.
2. **Search the whole market:** mega-cap/index familiarity, news volume, option liquidity, and analyst coverage do not earn alpha score. Aegis must actively search small/mid-cap and under-covered names.
3. **Human heuristic to formalize:** start broad, identify large expectation gaps, then ask 'what will the world need and who fulfills it?', look for catalysts/state changes, recent dislocations, and plausible causal upside. Analyst targets generate candidates; they are not truth.
4. **Anchor != expression:** NVDA/SPY/QQQ and other mega-caps can be sensors, causal anchors, benchmarks, or hedges. The best trade may be a less-famous supplier, bottleneck owner, loser, or second-order beneficiary.
5. **Data before story:** use primary point-in-time evidence where possible. Preserve vintages and source independence. LLMs propose causal hypotheses; deterministic/quant engines compute, backtest, risk, and grade.
6. **Fact before price:** for event studies, freeze the fundamental interpretation before revealing the market reaction whenever possible.
7. **Execution units matter:** evaluate strategies in the returns/costs/path mechanics an actual account receives. Never promote benchmark/log-return artifacts as tradeable alpha.
8. **Refusal and negative results are assets:** kill bad strategies quickly, record why, and use the corpse ledger to train/evaluate future research agents.
9. **Velocity-adaptive roadmap:** gates and dependencies outrank calendar estimates. If evidence gates pass in one session, advance immediately. Agent speed cannot replace prospective evidence or statistical power.
10. **Continuity:** every substantial session updates the handoff, roadmap state, killed/open hypotheses, exact next discriminating tests, and execution state. When Optimus Tier-2 project memory is available, ingest/distill these artifacts automatically so strategic intent is not lost between sessions.
11. **Night safety:** night discovers; day reviews/promotes. Night research must not mutate broker/execution state or silently change live paper loops.
12. **Long-term architecture:** WORLD SENSORS -> EVIDENCE -> PERSISTENT CAUSAL GRAPH -> MARKET EXPECTATIONS -> DIVERGENCE -> EXPRESSION -> ADMISSION -> REALITY -> LEARNING. The moat is accumulated evidence, causal calibration, execution discipline, and strategy allocation—not the LLM brand.
