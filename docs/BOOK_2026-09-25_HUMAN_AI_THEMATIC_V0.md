## 1. How "human + AI" is defined here, so the grade can attribute

| side | does | does not |
|---|---|---|
| **AI** (this draft) | turns each theme into candidates with a dated catalyst, the latest analyst action, the bear case, the one observation that separates the pick from sector beta, and a verdict; sizes by verdict and liquidity; declares cash | pick on a story without a dated observable; use target-level upside as a reason (it is measured perverse, §17) |
| **Human** (Murat) | vetoes, adds, resizes; supplies the instinct the AI cannot source (e.g. "the CEO is revolutionary") **as a typed hypothesis with a falsifier** | edit after the freeze |
| **Twins** (graded beside it) | `ew` same names equal-weight · `sector_etf` theme ETFs at the theme weights · `ai_only` this draft untouched · `spy` | — |

If the book beats `spy` but not `sector_etf`, the themes were right and the
picks added nothing. If it beats `sector_etf` but `ai_only` beats it, the human
edits cost money. If `ai_only` loses to `ew`, the AI's sizing was noise. Every
one of those is a result.

Horizons: 1 / 5 / 21 / 126 sessions, net of the empirical entry cost by
liquidity band (`llm_portfolio.grade`). Objective: **maximise 126-session
return vs SPY**; the competition variant (§6) has a different objective.
