## Read this before reading the table

**A floor is not an error.** It is the reading an instrument produces on
data containing *none* of what it measures. Every estimator has one;
almost none of them had been measured. The danger is not that the number
is wrong — it is that a reading below the floor is indistinguishable
from a real small value, and it is wrong in a *systematic direction*:
it OVER-states small quantities. That is how AGK came to over-charge
megacaps roughly tenfold while looking like a strict improvement.

**Every floor here is a LOWER bound.** Each is measured on a *declared*
synthetic microstructure that is kinder than the market in every
direction it simplifies: volume clustering, overnight gaps,
time-varying spreads and informed flow are all omitted, and every one of
them would raise the floor.

**A floor comes from the VARIANCE of the null reading, not its level.**
An instrument that always reads 3 too high is *biased* — subtract 3. One
whose null reading wanders over [0, 6] is *blind* below 6, and no
constant recovers it. The two need opposite remedies (recalibrate versus
replace), so the table reports the null band and the bias separately.

**The rule (Order 18 §4):** an instrument's floor is part of the
instrument. Below it, refusal — the floor value is never the estimate.
`instrument_floor.guard_reading()` enforces this with
`UNRESOLVABLE_FOR_INPUT`.

## What this sweep found

**1. The spread estimators differ by 9x in what they can see.** `agk_edge_spread` floors at 32bp; `roll_spread` at 280bp and cannot resolve anything below 400bp. Every one of these is used somewhere as "the spread". The AGK supersession is now MEASURED rather than cited: `agk_edge_spread` 32bp, `abdi_ranaldo_spread` 66bp, `corwin_schultz_spread` 79bp, `roll_spread` 280bp.

**2. The absorption ratio reads 0.42 on completely INDEPENDENT assets.** Its null is not zero — it is k/n plus sampling bias — so the LEVEL carries no information about coupling, and a report saying "absorption 0.42, markets tightly coupled" is reading noise. Its *changes* do resolve (from a 0.05 factor share), so the usable statistic is the movement, never the level.

**3. Not every instrument has a problem.** `realized_vol` reads 0.0200 against a null of 0.02 with a floor of 0.0213 — essentially no gap. It is in the table as the control: without one, a sweep that finds a floor everywhere is indistinguishable from a sweep whose harness manufactures floors.
