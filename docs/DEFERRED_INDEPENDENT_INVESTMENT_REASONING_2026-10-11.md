## Owner's clarification

AEGIS should form its own investment views from business economics, public developments, customer behaviour, competition and future events. Analyst opinions are valuable inputs and later comparison points, not permission to have a view. An old positive analyst consensus or strong past quarter must not automatically dismiss a new negative thesis; negative revisions must not automatically dismiss a well-supported turnaround thesis.

The owner wants an investor brain, not a news-summary archive or a system that merely waits for analysts to react. It should investigate whether it can identify useful implications before consensus changes, while also recognising that markets or analysts may already have reacted. Earlier detection is a hypothesis to measure, not an assumed advantage.

Immediate priority remains completing the existing beta, measured adaptive weighting, portfolio construction, reliable paper operations and the Bloomberg workflow. Do not divert this sprint into a new research subsystem.

## Intended reasoning chain

Business model and current valuation → verified new information → customer, competitor and management responses → effects on demand, retention, pricing, revenue, costs and cash flow → implications versus market expectations → horizon-specific paper decision → recorded outcome and learning.

Keep three things distinct: business quality, likely changes to the business, and the attractiveness of its stock at the current price. Also separate short-run sentiment/repricing from longer-run earnings effects. A five-week contest opportunity and a six-month investment thesis need not produce the same choice.

## Case hypotheses to preserve

These are the owner's research ideas, not verified descriptions of current conditions or recommendations. No fresh company research was performed to create this note.

- **Adobe / digital-product economics:** The original attraction is a repeatable digital product with scalable distribution and recurring income. Test whether capable free or cheaper AI alternatives weaken paid adoption, renewals, pricing power or customer lifetime value. Analyse freelancers and enterprise customers separately. Investigate the incumbent response: product integration, AI features, commercial assurances, support, distribution and workflow switching costs. Do not treat feature imitation as proof that a complete business is replicated. Distinguish publicly discussed backlash from measurable churn or revenue effects.
- **Roblox / platform growth and reputation:** Investigate durable engagement, demographic expansion, creator incentives and monetisation against safety controversies and regulation. Ask whether pessimism is temporary, whether protective measures change trust or engagement, and whether operating costs offset benefits. Do not assume a growing gaming market automatically creates profitable growth for this company.
- **Nike / brand-led turnaround:** Investigate whether a durable brand and customer habit can support recovery after a stock decline. Determine the actual reasons for weaker expectations: demand, product appeal, competition, distribution, inventory, discounting, costs or valuation. Separate temporary difficulties from structural erosion. Brand familiarity and distance from an old share-price peak do not settle the valuation question.
- **Take-Two / GTA franchise economics:** Evaluate launch cash generation, delivery risk, margins, recurring online monetisation and the durability of the franchise. Delivering an expected launch can reduce uncertainty and support value; do not automatically dismiss the thesis with “priced in.” Estimate what the current price actually assumes, then test the consequences of meeting, exceeding or missing those assumptions. Verify current dates before use.
- **Sony / gaming ecosystem:** Examine memory/component costs, hardware pricing, digital ownership versus subscriptions, consumer sentiment and the shift of gaming across platforms. For alleged emulation, reverse engineering or piracy developments, verify technical scope, compatibility, adoption and the overlap with otherwise-paying customers. Separate a headline-driven price reaction from material lost revenue; convenience, official access and platform services may affect substitution.
- **GoPro / substitution and pivots:** Test the owner's explanation involving smartphone-camera substitution, competition from action/360 cameras, specialist niches and a proposed pivot. Reconstruct dated company evidence and market reactions. Determine whether a rally reflects improved operating prospects, a changed narrative, positioning or some combination; do not assume the pivot caused the entire move.
- **AI disruption and defence across sectors:** Map cheaper product creation and security threats to potentially affected software, gaming and cybersecurity businesses. Evaluate incumbent responses and beneficiaries as well as losers. The owner's reference to NVIDIA buying Hugging Face is an unverified transaction premise in this note. Verify acquisition versus investment, partnership or rumour before using it in any causal chain. Do not store it as an established fact.

## How analyst evidence should be used

Record AEGIS's initial thesis, evidence, timestamp, available analyst view and prevailing price. Preserve that original view when later analyst revisions arrive. Ask whether the new analyst report contains genuinely new evidence, merely repeats the same root event, or contradicts the original view. Do not count several repetitions of one announcement as independent confirmation.

Track publication → AEGIS detection → thesis → paper decision → analyst revision, plus the price reaction at each stage. Evaluate agreement and disagreement cases. A useful independent view can be correct before consensus, correct without consensus ever changing, or wrong; all three belong in the learning record.

## Required outputs when this work is activated

An understandable investment note should explain what the company sells, why customers pay, what changed, who may switch or benefit, management's possible response, how the economics could change, what the price seems to assume, relevant future dates, opposing evidence, and what would invalidate the thesis. Quantified claims need sources or explicitly labelled assumptions.

Freeze the resulting paper decision and alternatives. Compare the independent-thesis approach with analyst-only and existing-engine baselines on matched dates, eligible instruments and costs. Grade the business forecast separately from stock return and decision usefulness. Report both successes and failures. Do not force positive weights or rewrite older forecasts after outcomes are known.

## Additional owner clarification: analyst lead/lag and later LLM interfaces

Analyst ratings, targets, prices and their explanations remain useful evidence. The owner hypothesises that an AEGIS view formed from new information might precede a later analyst revision and a useful price reaction. Preserve the initial decision, agreement and disagreement cases, and measure whether the subsequent reaction occurs. Same-root-event agreement is not independent confirmation; a positive revision does not guarantee a rise. Do not assume the market had not already reacted.

After beta, consider skills, a plugin or a Claude/GPT connector through which a larger daily reasoning LLM uses existing Optimus MCP capabilities and AEGIS engine evidence. Inspect existing capabilities first. This is a deferred interface idea, not an instruction to create another MCP/agent framework now, restart paused services, activate a paid route or grant real-money authority.
