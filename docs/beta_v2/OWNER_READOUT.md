# Beta V2: what the review means for the build

Your request is to make AEGIS turn evidence into better decisions, then learn
whether those decisions helped. The reports call for an evolving ATLAS world
model, dynamic policy siblings, broad research and a usable owner control loop.
They do not call for replacing the existing engine or erasing the static books.

All supplied originals are saved. Both reports, all 15 workbook sheets and the
recovered Consensus research have been read. The latest `AEGIS REVIEWS.txt`
contains only its heading; the substantive raw review and private ChatGPT export
are not in the supplied files. Older review material is preserved separately.

The newer broad-research instruction wins over Edition 02's tiny-pilot framing.
One end-to-end chain is useful as an integration check, but it should not serialize
all independent experiments behind a long horizon. Existing-data research can
proceed while WRDS authentication or account decisions wait.

Several tempting interpretations need stronger tests. More books do not imply
independent bets: compare holdings, return/signal correlation, root events and
ancestry. A price-only reconstruction is not a trade record: compare complete
exit/hedge/re-entry paths on event cohorts with fair costs. A graph link is not
causation: retain explicit co-mention and hypothesis types. A failed low-power
test does not prove absence of an edge: calibrate null and injected-edge behavior
before trusting wins or kills, while retaining the original closed-cell evidence.

The four-gate carrier issue is concrete engineering work: strict calibration and
financial modes must agree across native, shim, outer guard and PowerShell.
Same-package actual read-only calibration and independent audit are required
before financial mode; code tests alone cannot establish that authority.

The remaining owner-only steps are secure authentication/MFA, numeric Telegram
pairing, the broker role/alias/reset decision, and relevant contest/seed authority.
Technical thresholds with adequate evidence stay with the builders and reviewer.
The old coding-quota floor and Istanbul-host plan are superseded. Purchases and
unbounded inference spending are not included.

Current observations are dated receipts, not a declaration that V1/V1.1 or V2
has passed release acceptance.
