## 2. New external receipts banked this session

1. **Variance risk premium — killed at prior-check before we spent a
   registration.** Dew-Becker & Giglio, *The Decline of the Variance Risk
   Premium* (Chicago Fed WP 2025-17 / SSRN 5525882, Sep 2025): index-option
   returns had sharply negative alphas historically, but **over the past ~15
   years option alphas are indistinguishable from zero**; synthetic options
   never had negative alpha over 100 years. Consequence: covered-call /
   put-write / short-vol income (JEPI/XYLD/PUTW class) is **not a source of
   alpha** on modern data — it is a payoff-reshaping trade. Options-income was
   the most obvious untested family adjacent to our stack; it is now closed at
   the gate, unregistered, zero deflation cost. **This is what a working
   prior_check looks like.**
2. **Tax-loss harvesting is larger than any alpha we have confirmed.**
   Chaudhuri, Burnham & Lo, *An Empirical Evaluation of Tax-Loss-Harvesting
   Alpha* (FAJ 2020), 1926-2018 on the 500 largest names: **+1.10%/yr gross,
   +0.94% net of transaction costs; +0.85% / +0.69% under the wash-sale
   constraint.** Compare: our one confirmed cross-sectional survivor (gp-small)
   is +24 to +33 bps/mo at t 1.11–1.45 — i.e. *not* distinguishable from noise —
   and lives in small caps. **Caveat that decides the personal case: Hong Kong
   levies no capital gains tax.** For a HK-resident book the tax alpha is
   exactly zero. It is a *product* feature for US-taxable users, not a lever for
   Murat's own portfolio. Flagged so nobody builds it for the wrong reason.
3. **Insider-buy alpha has decayed 60–70% post-2010** off the Cohen-Malloy-
   Pomorski 82 bps/mo (JF 2012) baseline, to roughly 30–40 bps/mo, with 10b5-1
   usage and algorithmic trading compressing the disclosure window. Coherent
   with our own +102 bps/mo **t 1.89**. Add to TRIAL-CMP-INSIDER-IC as a
   declared adverse prior *before* the Oct refresh — it lowers what the forward
   IC has to beat to be unremarkable.
4. **Time-series momentum is weaker than the TSMOM-XA lane's registration
   implies.** Huang et al., *Time series momentum: is it there?* — pooled
   regressions overstate 12-month predictive ability; extended samples show
   negative test-period Sharpes for nearly all parameterisations on ETFs; ~40%
   of TSM returns are time-varying macro-factor exposure. **This does not touch
   the lane** (its claim is *shallower drawdowns*, Goal-B defensive, with return
   drag t −1.86 already disclosed) — but it should be appended to
   `TRIAL-TSMOM-XA.md` as an adverse prior so the 2028 decision is read against
   it. Registering the doubt now is free; registering it after the result is not.
5. **Residual / idiosyncratic momentum survives post-publication and avoids
   momentum crashes.** Blitz-Huij-Martens (JEmpFin 2011); Blitz-Hanauer-
   Vidojevic (*The idiosyncratic momentum anomaly*, IRFA 2020): comparable
   average returns at **~half the volatility**, robust across global universes,
   holds up out-of-sample after publication, and — the load-bearing part —
   **does not suffer the long-term reversal that produces momentum crashes.**
   See §3.3.

## 3. What is actually worth doing — ranked

### 3.1 The structural read (do this before anything else)

Murat's stated goal is "win at backtest, max ROI, beat SPY." Three receipts say
the first clause is the problem:

- **INSTR-OVERFIT-CEILING:** best honest-direction full-sample t our entire
  53-signal closed library can produce is **2.94** (top-5 composite 3.27), against
  a zero-skill expected maximum of **3.6–4.0**. Allow sign-flips and mining
  reaches t 6.16 / Sharpe 1.44 — a fake track record most funds would envy.
  **A great backtest is manufacturable here and we have measured the price list.**
- **Empty cost-killed cohort:** across 155 candidates, zero signals were killed
  by costs alone. Every rejection was informational — the edges were arbitraged
  away, not eaten by fees.
- **CZ-CALIB:** rank correlation between published t-stat and our measured t is
  **−0.544**. The more celebrated a signal, the deader it is on 2004-2018.

Conclusion, stated plainly for the paper and for Murat: **monthly-resolution
cross-sectional stock selection on the data we can reach is exhausted.** The
correct response is not a 160th candidate. It is (a) a new information axis, and
(b) accepting that the deliverable of the research program is the *method and the
graveyard*, while the deliverable of the *product* is risk-shaping and
decision-quality, not alpha.

### 3.2 The one genuinely open information axis — and it is on disk

**OptionMetrics.** WRDS batch-3b catalogued library `optionm` at **578 tables**,
including `opprcd1996`…`opprcd2025` (daily option prices), `opvold` (option
volume), `secprd` (underlying prices) — and it has never been touched. This is:

- a **different information class** from everything in the graveyard (which is
  price/volume, fundamentals, analyst, insider, text, macro);
- **daily resolution**, which is the only admissible successor class the house
  has left open for event families (§14, §16);
- backed by real priors that are not analyst-consensus-flavoured: implied-vol
  spread (call-put IV), the option-to-stock volume ratio (Roll-Schwartz-
  Subrahmanyam), realised-minus-implied vol, and IV skew as a cross-sectional
  predictor of equity returns;
- **long-only executable** — the options are the *input*; the trade is still a
  stock.

⚠️ **Caveat, stated so nobody plans on it:** `list_tables` succeeding proves the
catalog is visible, **not** that read entitlement exists. Step zero is one cheap
test query (`SELECT * FROM optionm.opprcd2015 LIMIT 10`) on the next Duo tap.
If it 403s, this whole branch is dead and should be recorded as such.

Second axis, cheaper and already half-owned: **full daily CRSP (`crsp.dsf`)** for
the general universe. We have `dsf_pharma` only. The 8-K and PEAD families are
both closed at monthly resolution with the *explicit* note that a daily event
harness is the only admissible successor — and that harness cannot be built
without daily returns for the whole universe.
