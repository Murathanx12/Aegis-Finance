## 2. The objection worth running — residual momentum (Gemini)

**Claim:** *"A 36-month rolling OLS in small caps suffers severe beta estimation
risk. Subtracting β̂′F injects high-variance sampling noise that dominates the
residual. You didn't kill idiosyncratic momentum; you measured the estimation
error of rolling 36-month OLS in illiquid names."*

This is the strongest criticism received in two rounds: specific, mechanistic,
and it makes predictions opposite to ours. Run as a diagnostic
(`scripts/diag_resid_mom_decomp.py`; the REJECT stands either way — nothing is
scored against a bar, no variant can graduate, the only question is *why*).

**Test A — decompose the same 11-month window three ways, identical mean/sd
form, same 36-month betas:**

| component (small caps, explore) | mean IC | **t(IC)** |
|---|---|---|
| total = mean(excess)/sd | 0.0219 | 2.84 |
| **fitted = mean(β′F)/sd — the tilt** | **0.0208** | **2.80** |
| residual = mean(e)/sd — resid_mom | 0.0030 | 0.58 |

The tilt leg alone reproduces essentially all of total momentum's rank
information, and carries **75.9%** of the score's cross-sectional variance.
Largemid agrees (fitted 1.22, residual 0.11).

**Test B — dose response. If beta noise is the executioner, reducing it must
restore the IC:**

| spec | parameters | small-cap t(IC) |
|---|---|---|
| FF3 / 36m (frozen) | 4 | 0.58 |
| CAPM / 36m — half the parameters | 2 | **0.49** |
| FF3 / 60m — two-thirds more observations | 4 | **1.13** |

Halving the parameter count made it marginally worse. The 60-month arm moved
0.58 → 1.13 — **a real partial concession, disclosed: some estimation noise is
present.** But it recovers a fraction of the 2.80 the tilt carries alone and
stays far under the 2.0 bar.

**Verdict: tilt confirmed, estimation-noise refuted as the primary cause.** The
finding is stronger than when it was a single null — small-cap total momentum's
2004-2018 cross-sectional information is *reproducible from the factor-tilt
component alone*. Credit where due: this is the objection that improved a result
rather than restating one. Logged into NEGATIVE_RESULTS §23.

## 4. New finding produced by the audit — NEGATIVE_RESULTS §24

The flow-signal sweep was run to answer a criticism and produced a result worth
stating on its own. Across 19 flow candidates, differencing **systematically
raises rank information and destroys tradability**:

- The three highest IC t-stats in the entire 159-candidate library are flow
  signals — `text_jac` 7.47, `text_cos` 6.53, `si_chg_low` 6.09.
- Almost all have net-dead books, because differencing drives one-way turnover
  to 0.3-0.7/month (`conn_mom` 0.682, `cust_mom` 0.709, `si_chg_low` 0.457).
- The only flows with a positive net t anywhere are the three low-turnover ones
  (`comp_issue_5y` 0.062, `net_issuance_low` 0.078, `fscore_lite` 0.129).

Same shape as the program's central result, from a new angle: **the information
is real and the premium is not collectible.** Usable as a cheap screening rule —
a flow construction with one-way turnover above ~0.15/month should be expected
to die net regardless of its IC, and a high IC is not evidence against that.

## 5. The genuinely new idea — and why it inherits an existing blocker

**Gemini's Schedule 13D/13G proposal is the one item in four reviews with no
prior registration.** Verified: zero hits across all 45 registry rows, all trial
docs, STATUS, the factory protocol and the taxonomy. It appears twice in
`docs/PROJECT_LANDSCAPE.md` / `docs/BACKLOG.md` as an unbuilt backlog line noting
it is cheap — *">5% stake events on EXISTING `_sec_get` plumbing — just a
form-type filter."*

The economic argument is sound: unlike 13F's 45-day quarterly lag, a 13D must be
filed within 10 days of crossing 5%, and activist 13Ds carry explicit operational
catalysts.

**But it is an event family, and this program has a receipt about event families
at monthly resolution.** NEGATIVE_RESULTS §20 records exactly what happens: the
distress-8-K study recovered eligibility selection rather than filing
information, and its control arm beat its treatment arm. §14 and §16 close PEAD
and FDA drift at monthly cadence with the explicit note that **a daily-resolution
event harness is the only admissible successor class.** Running 13D/13G monthly
would reproduce the §20 error with a new form type.

**Adjudication: 13D/13G is ADMISSIBLE and QUEUED, and it inherits the
`crsp.dsf` blocker.** It is a daily-event-harness candidate, not a monthly scan.
That makes full daily CRSP the gating dependency for *three* families now
(PEAD, 8-K, 13D/13G) rather than two — which raises its priority above the
OptionMetrics entitlement test in my judgement, since one acquisition unblocks
three registrations.

Gemini's other structural proposals are partly pre-adjudicated: index
inclusion/rebalancing is a **data-blocked stub** (no PIT membership or
announcement dates on disk) and the effect has decayed sharply — direct
additions +5.4% vs migrations −1.8% in the 2010s, migrations rising from ~50% to
>70% of additions, demand multiplier down roughly 20×. One honest nuance from
the same sweep: **Russell reconstitution is not cleanly certified dead** (the
change is not statistically significant). Spin-offs / forced institutional
selling remain a queued batch-3 leftover, blocked on corporate-event data.

## 6. GPT's reframe — admissible, unqueued, and the most interesting thing said

GPT's core argument is that the program has exhausted *individual information
classes* but not *relationships between them*, and proposes searching

```
signal → change in another signal
```

instead of `signal → return`: options flow predicting insider buying, 13F
predicting analyst revisions, earnings language predicting option skew — an
"information propagation engine" whose transitions are each measurable and
pre-registerable.

**This is not in the graveyard and is not refuted by anything in it.** It is
also honestly assessed as expensive: every arrow needs its own PIT source, its
own control arm, and its own deflation slot, and the program's own experience
(§20, TEXT-LAZY) is that *the control arm is where these designs die*. A
propagation chain multiplies the number of places a control arm is needed.

Recorded as **admissible, unqueued, post-freeze**. If it is ever built, the
first arrow tested should be the one with the cheapest control, not the most
interesting story.

One correction to its framing: GPT writes that the analog engine's failure
"does not imply historical analogs contain no information — it implies this
embedding contains no resolution." That is right, and it is what the trial
concluded; the D1-D4 record explicitly scopes the null to the tested
representation and names a successor path (a new walled registration starting
from fwd-12m, where RES/UNC was 29.3%). The briefing's summary was terser than
the underlying record. Fair hit on the summary, not on the work.
