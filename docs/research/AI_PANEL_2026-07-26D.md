## GPT's architectural proposal — adopted as the REGIME-ANALOG design spec

The "state estimation / belief engine" framing is right-sized and adopted
for the allocation program's design (build next):

- **State estimation, not prediction:** the engine answers "what market
  are we living in" (distribution change detection), never "what happens
  tomorrow."
- **Analog retrieval (Build 4 core):** k-NN over a wide daily macro
  descriptor vector (VIX, MOVE-proxy, credit, curve, dollar, oil/gold,
  sector RS, breadth, insider flow) → 50 nearest historical periods →
  forward distributions at 3/6/12/24m horizons, sector/factor winners,
  crash frequency. Deterministic, auditable.
- **Belief states with confidence trajectories:** the Brain stores
  {state probabilities, confidence, supporting evidence list, timestamp}
  and tracks how beliefs evolve — a new artifact class beside trials
  (descriptive, never-arm until a rule passes the wall).
- **LLM narrates; deterministic engine allocates** (unchanged CANON).
- Evidence graphs (oil→inflation→Fed→rates→growth chains): phase 2, after
  the analog layer exists — chains without retrieval are just priors.
