**FKP's own Table 2** reports a *"Buffett-Style Long-Only Portfolio, Leverage =
1.00"*: excess return **8.4%** vs market **7.5%** — **+0.9pp/yr gross**, on the

**Our own receipt that condemns it:** the **−0.544** fame-vs-performance

## 2. Regime-conditional commodity overlay — **DEAD** (this session recommended it; it was wrong)

The source is real — Jacobsen, Marshall & Visaltanachoti, *Management Science*
65(7) 2019 — and the −1.5%/+0.5% sign flip is verbatim in the abstract. It dies
on two other grounds.

**Base rate.** Goyal, Welch & Zafirov (*RFS* 2024) retested 29 post-2008 predictors
plus the original 17. **Tally V: 0 of 17 monthly-frequency variables were still
both in-sample and out-of-sample significant by 2020. Tally VII: 45 of 45
underperformed unconditional buy-and-hold; 20 of 45 lost money outright.** The
nearest testable analog — Driesprong-Jacobsen-Maat's oil predictor, *same lead
author, same channel, same frequency* — passed OOS in its own sample and then
**failed on extension**.

**The decisive kill is structural: the regime state is not knowable in real time,
and the confirm window has no recession in it.** NBER announcement lags: mean peak
**7.2 months**, trough **15.2**, overall **11.2**. The 2007-09 peak was announced
2008-12-01, after 11 of 18 recession months had passed; the 2020 peak was announced
**two months after the recession had already ended.**

Then the arithmetic on our own split: explore 2004-2018 has **18 recession months
(10.0%)**, all one episode; confirm 2019-2024 has **2 (Mar–Apr 2020, 2.8%)**, one
episode, in a state not declared until 2021. **The recession arm — the entire
sign-flip claim — has n=2 in confirm.** Any confirm result would be 100% the
expansion branch. There is no version of this that is confirmable on our wall.

**Our own receipts:** NEG_RESULTS §15 — the jump model passed all three explore
bars and died at confirm, verdict *"one crisis (2008) wearing three bars as a
disguise."* This would be attempt three at regime rotation with a commodity
instead of a moving average.

## 3. Weekly negative-news exclusion — **DEAD**

**The source paper's own Table 6 destroys the thesis.** Heston & Sinha (*FAJ*
2017), decomposing the 13-week drift:

| Component | 13-week cumulative | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-earnings** | **0.25%** | **not significant** |
| Earnings week | 5.57% | t=2.7 |
| Post-earnings | 1.83% | t=2.1 |

Their words: news sentiment *"does not predict statistically significant returns
prior to firms' next earnings announcement."* **The "long delayed reaction to
negative news" is PEAD wearing a news costume** — and PEAD has been *"non-existent
since 2006"* for large stocks (Martineau, *CFR*), inside Heston & Sinha's own
2003-2010 window. **Our own `pead_agree` came out INVERTED** (IC t −2.6, both
segments).

Two *JF* papers say the news effect is days-and-reverses: Tetlock 2007 (+1SD
pessimism −8.1bp day 1, +6.8bp reversal, **net 5-day −1.3bp, insignificant**;
cost cutoff 4.4bp) and Tetlock-Saar-Tsechansky-Macskassy 2008 (*"no longer
profitable after accounting for reasonable levels of transaction costs — e.g.,
10 bps"*).

**And exclusion screens have a measured price:** Hong & Kacperczyk (*JFE* 2009) —
shunned stocks earn **+26 bps/month** four-factor alpha, ~2.5%/yr, paid by the
excluders.

**Our own §20 receipt:** the 8-K placebo control beat the treatment because
eligibility conditions on survival-to-event. A negative-news screen is the same
design with a fuzzier trigger.

## 5. Ultra-low-turnover concentrated quality — **WOUNDED** (and it caught my error)

**I misread our own receipt.** INSTR-HOLD-HORIZON's flat 14-17 bps/mo across
1-24 month bands is **conditional on signal-band exits** — the gloss is literally
*"sell when the name exits the band, not on calendar/stop-loss."* That is a
state-dependent exit with long average duration, **not** a static multi-year book.
Candidate 5 dropped the condition and kept the conclusion. That is a category
error and it was mine. Corroborating: Bowles-Reed-Ringgenberg-Thornock (*JF*
2024), *"anomaly returns are concentrated in the first month after information
release dates, and these returns decay soon thereafter."*

**Skew arithmetic.** Bessembinder (*JFE* 2018), 20,000-rep bootstrap on 25,967
CRSP names — a **25-stock** portfolio beats the value-weighted market **48.7%** of
years, **45.4%** of decades, **36.8%** of 90-year spans, *"even without any
deduction for fees or trading costs."*

**Measurement problem.** At FKP's own unlevered long-only IR of 0.45, t=2 needs
**~20 years**; Harvey's t>3 needs **~44 years**. It cannot be distinguished from
luck within the operator's research lifetime.

**What survives:** it is the only candidate on the right side of Novy-Marx &
Velikov's <50%-one-sided-monthly-turnover boundary — the most robust cost finding
in the literature. At ~20%/yr turnover and our measured 3-4bp largemid spreads,
cost drag is **~5 bps/yr, genuinely negligible.** Costs are not this candidate's
problem; **alpha and measurability are.**

introducing lookahead bias that compromises the validity of backtests."*
