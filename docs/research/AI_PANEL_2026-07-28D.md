### Why the knowledge graph is refused

Not scale-phobia — **we already measured the edges.** A supplier/customer graph
propagates effects along links whose cross-sectional information content we have
tested directly:

- `TRIAL-THEME-SUPPLY` basket arm: **REJECT**, B−A spread **t = 0.10** (+3.2
  bps/mo) — no cross-sectional information at annual cadence.
- `cust_mom` (customer momentum): **REJECT**.
- `conn_mom` (Ali-Hirshleifer connected momentum, literature 1.68%/mo at t 9.67):
  **net t −0.78** on 67% monthly turnover — the starkest paper-vs-cost gap we
  have measured.
- `cust_conc`: sign reversal, book-inspected clean (IC t −7.4).

The first-order links carry nothing tradable at our cadence. **Second-order
effects along those same edges are weaker, not stronger** — GPT's oil → airlines
→ tourism → aircraft chain multiplies four link coefficients we have measured at
approximately zero. Adding a graph would not add information; it would add
degrees of freedom, which `INSTR-OVERFIT-CEILING` prices at **t 6.58 from zero
skill**. This also re-affirms the round-8 refusal of vector DBs / Neo4j at a
~50-document corpus (revisit ~500).

## 2. Why GPT's replay proposal is blocked — by GPT's own criterion

GPT asks for a day-by-day replay in which the Brain sees only information
available on, say, 2020-03-12, forms beliefs, and advances. He then names the
right acceptance test: *"If a new component can't beat its simpler baseline under
point-in-time testing, remove it."*

**Those two requirements are jointly unsatisfiable for the news layer, and the
second one is the reason.** A PIT reasoning replay needs a survivorship-clean
point-in-time news archive covering the test window. Measured this round:
Bigdata/RavenPack has **no individual archive tier at all** — public news is a
**5-year rolling window**; Massive at $29/mo reaches 2016; Benzinga at $99/mo
reaches 2001 but consumes the entire budget. None reaches a 2004 explore start.
Independently, scoring pre-2015 text with any modern LLM is documented
contamination (Kelly, Malamud, Schwab & Xu, NBER w35247: LLMs *"inevitably embed
information from the future"*), and Gao, Jiang & Yan show LLM predictive power is
amplified on memorized firm-date pairs and **loses significance
post-training-cutoff**.

So the news/reasoning layer **cannot be held to GPT's own standard**. It is
therefore **forward-only** — descriptive, informing Murat's manual conviction
decisions, never entering the pre-registration pipeline. That is not a
concession; it is the standard being applied honestly to the one component that
cannot meet it.

The *structured* half of the replay — macro state vectors, EDGAR events, PIT
fundamentals — is exactly `INSTR-REGIME-ANALOG` and is already queued.

**ADOPTED as standing rule S6:** every new Brain component ships with a named
simpler baseline and a PIT comparison against it, or it does not ship. Applies to
REGIME-ANALOG phase 1 first.

## 3. Gemini — one demand, one question

**The demand (sever `pandas_datareader`): DONE for the path that matters.**
`587bbe1` pins the research harness: `data/ff_factors.parquet` sha256
`54e3b8dd…`, 1963-07 → 2026-05, 755 months, vintage date ≤ 2026-07-21 recorded
honestly as UNKNOWN-download-date rather than backfilled. `load_ff_factors`
verifies the hash on **every** load and raises on mismatch. The cache was already
an accidental pin; the defect was that nothing recorded *which* vintage and
deleting the file would have silently re-derived every registry alpha on
different numbers with no error.

**Partial disagreement on scope.** All three reviewers also want the live product
surface (`backend/services/factor_model.py:57-58`) hard-pinned. **Declined as
stated.** That surface is a *live descriptive attribution*; pinning it freezes
factor data at the pin date and makes every trailing-window regression
progressively wrong — a real cost none of the reviews priced. The correct fix
there is a **retrieval stamp**, not a pin, so nothing can be lifted from the
product into the paper unstamped. Recorded as a follow-up; it is not
paper-blocking now that the research path is pinned. Flagged plainly rather than
quietly done differently.

**The question, answered:** *what isolates the TEXT-LAZY result from triggering a
full-engine re-evaluation before the freeze?*

Four mechanisms, all structural rather than procedural:

1. **The module is not the engine.** `Aegis module` is offline research with its
   own venv and gitignored `data/`. It has no import path into
   `aegis-finance/backend`, no scheduler hook, and no lane. A trial result cannot
   reach the product because there is no wire.
2. **Graduation is not adoption.** A confirm pass earns *forward-clock
   candidacy*, attended. Lane seeding is env-gated and a session never flips a
   seed flag. TEXT-LAZY rejected, so this never engaged.
3. **The registry is append-only and count-monotonic.** A rejection increments no
   candidate count (still 158) and rewrites nothing. There is no path by which a
   result revises earlier results.
4. **Results are written once.** The trial doc's Result section is marked "filled
   in AFTER the run — never edited afterwards." Re-evaluation would be an edit,
   and edits are visible in git.

The honest answer to the spirit of the question: **the risk was never
re-evaluation, it was the fallback trigger** — a one-sided threshold with no
control arm that would have carried the rejection forward into a *new*
registration. That is what §4 of the freeze note now bans.
