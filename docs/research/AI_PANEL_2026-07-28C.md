## 2. What changed my mind

**One thing.** I had the stacked-combination candidate (#4 in the red-team set) as
the natural way to use several individually-marginal edges. The Novy-Marx result
makes that substantially worse than I had it: the deflation must be combinatorial,
so stacking three marginal edges chosen from 158 searched candidates needs a bar
far above what any of them clears individually. **Candidate #4 is downgraded from
"promising synthesis" to "probably arithmetic suicide"** pending verification of
the citation.

That matters because "combine existing ideas" was the explicit instruction. The
honest answer is: combination is not free, and here it is expensive.
