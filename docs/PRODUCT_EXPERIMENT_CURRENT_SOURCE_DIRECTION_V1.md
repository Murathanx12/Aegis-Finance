# Current source direction cohort, v1 (prospective product experiment)

This is an opt-in multi-event exploratory cohort. An independently reviewed
source-context capture establishes source facts only. AEGIS separately declares
the ticker, H5 or H20 probability of beating SPY, rationale, contradiction,
source-fact references, author and time before sealing. The probability is an
unlearned AEGIS prior; the article supplies no stock-return probability. Each
case also needs the existing US issuer-table mapping, a dated adjusted US SIP
price-panel witness, and a prior completed price bar. This is an internal US
mapping plus panel check; it does not invent a primary-exchange listing proof.
The candidate pins source, review, hypothesis, mapping and panel hashes in its
immutable input snapshot. Entry must follow the seal in the same
UTC session. A changed case after sealing is refused.

`backend.services.current_event_forecast_bridge.prepare_candidate` performs
the admission and returns one H5/H20 `PRODUCT_EXPERIMENT` direction forecast
candidate. It also binds a separately pinned operator-approved H21 virtual
consumer receipt and independent review for the same scheduled entry, then
freezes its real holdings as a matched
with/without view. H5/H20 probability has no qualified mapping to H21 expected
excess return, so the signal is not applied and both views are identical:
HOLD. This is an observed consumer boundary, not a simulated news tilt. It
does not call a model, append a forecast, alter the existing digest, trade, or
attribute expected return. An independent review must precede any separate
`belief_state.append` call.

The CLI accepts one private JSON packet (`item`, `capture`, `semantic_review`,
`hypothesis`, `instrument`, `seal_utc`, `entry_after_utc`)
and writes one new
candidate receipt with `--packet`, `--trusted-manifest`, and `--output`. The
article packet cannot supply or replace the approved consumer. It reads the existing ledger
to refuse a changed case ID and uses the existing native base book. An output
path that already exists is refused. The caller must prepare the packet from
actual source and panel receipts, then declare and seal the AEGIS hypothesis
after this contract is committed and before the proposed entry. No backdating
or replacing an earlier native digest forecast is permitted. A new candidate
requires an observed seal within 120 seconds of creation and an entry still in
the future at creation. An exact already-sealed ledger row may be read back
later, but no new historical row may be minted.

This cohort is not an inferential trial or an alpha claim. Existing
`MISSING_POWER_FIELDS` and PILOT44 refusal remain unchanged. For evaluation,
count independent source events and matched controls, not forecast rows; retain
losses, costs and outcome timing. No result is promoted on these mechanics.

## Standalone prospective accrual, October 15 successor

The separate `source_context_direction_standalone_v1` mechanism preserves the
paired pilot above. It may observe H5/H20 forecasts without claiming an H21
return or portfolio allocation. The owner explicitly authorizes exploratory
multi-event paper observations while inferential power remains unqualified.
`MISSING_POWER_FIELDS` is retained for inference; it is never labelled PASS.
No forecast or account is adopted because this product workflow operates.

Source facts and AEGIS hypotheses remain separate. The first declared cohort
uses WINA's reported quarter/nine-month comparisons and SFBS's repurchase
authorization, with unlearned near-neutral probabilities 0.52/0.51 at H20.
FSBC's calendar announcement supplies context only and no directional forecast.
The family may accrue other independently supported, separately declared cases;
cases are counted by root event, with horizons and shared issuers clustered.

Primary metric: prospective Brier score minus the matched 0.50 control, by
declared horizon. Return/cost attribution is separate and never inferred from
moving a probability. Earliest skill review is October 15, 2028, subject to at
least 2,000 unique matured events and independently measured power/dependence.
Until then, or if power remains missing, disposition is UNQUALIFIED; losing
records remain. A confidence interval whose lower bound is not positive for
control-minus-candidate Brier prevents promotion. No interim winner selection,
backfilled source, or changed metric may be reported as confirmatory evidence.

The information seal must be actually observed. Entry is the next recent
broker-clock-verified regular US session, with source/price/identity hashes,
not a fabricated UTC seal. Closed SIP prices must retain the native adjustment
basis. The resolver starts at the declared entry-session close and refuses
missing entry, unmatched issuer/SPY dates, or future window ends. The generous
due-date rule stays unchanged. No broker order authority follows from this path.
On a data defect, hold the affected records and restart the affected prospective
cohort only with a new version. A SPY drawdown of 20% defers any skill decision
to at least six months after the trough. November 10 is a descriptive reporting
checkpoint; later outcomes are still graded after it.
