## Primary metric and decision rule (one rule for every cell, `hyp_cells.verdict`)

The design fold fixes the sign and the effect worth having; the confirm fold decides.
`CONDITIONAL_POSITIVE`: confirm mean > 0, t >= 2 and design mean > 0. `FAILED_VARIANT`: confirm mean <= 0,
or t < 2 with MDE <= max(design mean, the cell's minimum effect). Otherwise `CANNOT_DISTINGUISH`.
SE over date blocks (months for macro shocks, weeks otherwise); MDE = 2.8 x SE; by year/month and
leave-one-out worst on every receipt.

is expected by chance about one time in five.
