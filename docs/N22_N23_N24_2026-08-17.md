**2026-08-17, Order 10.** All three run entirely on the already-spent selection
window plus counts of months and names. No outcome was consumed, which is why

## N22 — the confirmation is not pending. It is unavailable.

M4 refused to spend the reserved window on a *return* effect. §59 says risk
resolves ~30× sooner, so the same question had to be asked of the statistic the
product actually claims.

**Answer: 0 of 8 cells resolvable.** Not one policy × outcome combination clears
its own MDE on the 74 reserved months — at the declared α 0.025 *or* the laxer
0.05 that M4 used. A ruling that flipped with the α choice would be a ruling
about α; this one does not.

```
  policy                 outcome        eff@74   SE@74   MDE.05  MDE.025
  voltarget_15_cap1.0    volatility      -2.84    1.60     4.48     4.94   NOT resolvable
  voltarget_15_cap1.0    drawdown        -6.09    5.63    15.77    17.36   NOT resolvable
                                          ... 0 of 8 resolvable ...
```

### The methodological correction it forced

`sizing_layer_risk_outcome.py` projected forward power the way M4 did:

```
    mde_forward = mde_full * sqrt(n_full / n_forward)
```

and compared it against the **full-window** effect. That is right for a mean —
the SE shrinks with √n and the mean does not care how long the window is.

**Max drawdown is not a mean. It is a path extremum and it grows with the path.**

| outcome | effect @ 14 yrs | effect @ 74 months | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| volatility | −2.91 | −2.84 | **0.97** |
| max drawdown | −19.18 | −6.09 | **0.32** |

Carrying a fourteen-year drawdown difference into a six-year standard error
credits the confirmation with two thirds of an effect it could never observe.
This run measures the forward quantity **on the forward horizon** instead — 74-
month circular-block sub-paths, effect and SE both read off them.

### And the constraint no standard error sees (§39)

The selection window crosses 20% drawdown **once**. At that rate, 74 months
expects **0.44** such episodes. A drawdown claim is a claim about crises, and
the reserved window should not be expected to contain one.

> The 85% of bootstrap sub-paths that reach 20% is **not** 85% of real 74-month
> windows. It measures how thoroughly 2008 is reused across resamples of a
> window that contains it once. The rate is the honest number, and it is below
> one.

**Ruling: the risk claim is PERMANENTLY SCREEN-GRADE on this corpus.** This is
strictly stronger than M4's "not on this candidate", and it changes the page:
`EXPLORE` alone reads as *confirmation pending*, and nothing is pending.

## N23 — the population sweep, and the expectation it refutes

`std_turn`'s two-names-per-month was found by accident. Run deliberately across
all eleven BH-FDR survivors:

```
  predictor                all   illiq     mid  liquid  liq dec  liq mo  verdict
  std_turn                 542     470      66       2        0       0  NO_POPULATION_IN_SCOPE
  ShareIss5Y              1862     563     603     688       69     167  HAS_POPULATION
  ... nine more, all 510-772 names in the liquid tercile ...
```

**One of eleven, and it is the one we already knew about.** The expectation that
this would cut the eleven further is not supported: the other ten have 510–772
eligible names per month in the tercile they claim to trade, scoreable in 167 of
168 months.

That is worth having in the other direction. It means the library's verdicts are
**effect-size and liquidity-concentration verdicts, not population artefacts** —
the conclusion stands on its own terms rather than on a universe that quietly
was not there.

### Made structural

`backend/services/research_gym/population.py`. The guard takes **per-period
counts** and derives the median itself; handed a declared number it has no
parameter to accept one, and handed nothing it raises. A test asserts the
absence of any such parameter, because that is the honour-system input that
produced the phantom cell in the first place.

Three verdicts: `HAS_POPULATION` · `THIN_POPULATION` · `NO_POPULATION_IN_SCOPE`.

**A dead branch, caught by its own test.** The first cut defined thin as
`share < 0.5`, which is unreachable: the median clears the requirement if and
only if at least half the periods do, so the two conditions are the same
condition and `THIN_POPULATION` could never be returned. The threshold is now
0.90 — a design unscoreable in a tenth of its declared periods is being measured
on a different universe from the one its table names — and a test asserts all
three verdicts are reachable.

## N24 — bound the sacrifice, and the answer is *no*

*"Not established"* is an `mde_mean` statement: our instrument could not separate
the estimate from zero. It says nothing about what has been **excluded**, and
exclusion is what a decision needs. So: the upper one-sided 95% bound on the
return **drag** against the break-even the variance reduction is worth.

**Shipped configuration (`voltarget_15_cap1.0`), at both declared λ:**

| λ | break-even | UCB(drag) | verdict | short by | extra data |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 0.81 %/yr | +2.93 %/yr | NOT_DEMONSTRATED | 2.12pp | +128 yrs |
| 3.0 | 2.42 %/yr | +2.93 %/yr | NOT_DEMONSTRATED | **0.51pp** | **+6 yrs** |

The bound does **not** rescue the claim. The honest sentence stays "not
established" — but it is now *bounded* rather than merely unresolved, and at
λ = 3 the gap is 0.51pp and about six more years, not the ~95 a return claim
needs. §59's ratio a third time.

### The trap in the table, named before anyone walked into it

Two non-shipped cells came back `RULED_OUT` (`voltarget_20_cap1.0` and
`voltarget_15_cap1.5`, both at λ = 3). **The product does not move to one of
them.** `voltarget_15_cap1.0` was the declared configuration before any of this
was computed; switching to whichever cell cleared is selection on the outcome —
§37's shape with an equivalence test wearing it.

Note *why* they clear, too: a weaker de-risking has a smaller variance reduction
**and** a smaller drag, and both happen to have a positive measured return
change, which is the noisy part of the comparison pushing the bound down.

And the machinery runs the other way. `voltarget_10_cap1.0` at λ = 1 returned
`AT_LEAST_MARGIN`: the point estimate of the drag (+1.45%/yr) already exceeds
what its variance reduction is worth (1.17%/yr). **A bound that could only ever
say "worth it" would not be a bound.**

`λ*` — the risk aversion at which each policy's bound would clear — is reported
as a diagnostic and explicitly not a recommendation. The four personalities are
declared preferences, and choosing one because it clears a bound turns them into
four more strategy parameters.
